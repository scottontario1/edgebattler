# Hollow Court: design reference

Supplied source art (pixel-style, transparent background) for the Hollow Court units in `FACTIONS.md` section 7. Never edited; `tools/assets/prep_sprites.py` trims and scales copies into `public/sprites/` when these become runtime sprites. Until then the Court classes use a tinted base sprite (see `docs/CULTURE_HOOKS.md`).

| file | unit | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `necromancer_sprite.png` | Necromancer | tall court official, silver-grey hair, red eyes, black velvet robe over grey silk with gold embroidery and tassels, staff with a hanging candle lantern, open book |
| `mourning_knight_sprite.png` | Mourning Knight | mounted knight in polished steel with a closed helm, purple mourning scarf and torn plume, spectral blue wisps and glowing eye slit, lance, kite shield with a laurel crest, horse in tattered purple barding |
| `feral_ghoul_sprite.png` | Feral Ghoul | hunched, pale grey skin, one glowing white eye, long clawed hands, torn brown rags with red sashes and wrappings |

Style notes for the rest of the faction: black, ash grey and steel with purple and pale gold accents; cold blue-white spectral glow for the undead touch, candle-amber for the living court; torn cloth edges and worn heraldry rather than gore; pixel-art outline weight as in these three.
